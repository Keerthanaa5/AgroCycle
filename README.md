# AgroCycle 🌱

Smart Agri-Business Platform for Profit Optimization

📌 Overview

AgroCycle is a smart agri-business platform designed to help farmers maximize income by transforming crop losses into profitable opportunities using AI-driven insights and market connections.

Farmers often face financial losses due to crop damage, waste burning, and limited market access. AgroCycle addresses these challenges by converting waste into value, promoting sustainability, and enabling multiple revenue streams.

🚀 Key Features
AgroConnect Community

Exchange crop waste, fodder & manure with your community to reduce costs and improve resource utilization.

🏭 Urban Waste Matcher

Sell damaged crops to hotels, industries & buyers (such as biofuel and processing units) instead of wasting them.

🌍 Carbon Cash

Farmers earn money by avoiding crop burning and adopting eco-friendly practices.
Companies that emit carbon support farmers financially to reduce emissions and gain carbon credit benefits.

🔍 Viability Scanner

Upload crop images and get AI-based analysis to identify issues and improve decision-making.

🐄 Silage Bank Network

Convert waste crops into animal feed at nearby centers, reducing loss and generating income.

⚡ Claim Rocket

Fast insurance claims with AI-powered crop damage assessment.

🌾 Intercrop Wizard

AI-powered crop suggestions to grow additional crops alongside main crops and increase income.
